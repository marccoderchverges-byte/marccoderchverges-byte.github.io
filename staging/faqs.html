FAQS
How does it work?

We’ve designed and patented a solution that makes stones touch sensitive and, thus, are able to turn on lights wirelessly.

Does it work with all stones?

As of today, volcanic stones remain the only one which we’ve not been able to work with. All other stones have worked perfectly.

Can I send my own stone?

That’s an awesome plan. We love to work with your stones. Just send us an email so we can check that it fits.

What type of products do you have?

Depending on the size of the stone the electronics are either inserted inside or in a wooden base. For more complex projects please reach out through email.

Where do you ship from?

This is designed by us in Barcelona. Paella and stones. Rock and Roll.

Installation

To install, simply unplug your lamp, plug it to the Pedra Plug®, and then plug it back again — next is magic!
