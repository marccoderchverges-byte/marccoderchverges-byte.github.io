What data we collect

  • Contact data you give us directly — your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message — when you email or call us.
  • Order and delivery data — your name, shipping address and order details — if and when you place an order through the Shop.
  • Anonymous, aggregate visit statistics collected through GoatCounter, a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not record personal data or full IP addresses.
  • Local browser storage that never reaches us — used only to keep the Shop unlocked for your current visit and to avoid repeating the same photo on the homepage. This stays on your device.

Why we use it

We use contact data to answer your enquiries. We use order data to process, dispatch and account for your purchase, and to meet our legal and tax obligations. We use anonymous analytics to understand how the site is used, without identifying individual visitors.

Payment processing

Payments are handled directly by Stripe, our payment processor. We do not see or store your card details — they are collected and processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. Order and invoicing data is kept for the period required by Spanish tax and commercial law.

Who we share it with

We share order data with Stripe (payment processing) and with the couriers used to ship your order. We share no personal data with anyone else, and we do not sell personal data.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site analytics (GoatCounter) are cookie-free.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer your personal data, or object to how we use it. To do so, email marc.coderch.verges@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD.
